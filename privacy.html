Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our application ("the App") handles information in connection with Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads. The App is operated by Harbir Gill ("we", "us", or "our").

What the App Does

The App is a webhook integration that receives lead notifications from Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads through the Meta platform. It acts solely as a technical pass-through service that forwards lead information to the associated business so they can respond to the inquiry.

The App does not provide a user interface, user accounts, or direct interaction with end users.

Data We Receive

When a user submits a lead form through Facebook or Instagram advertisements, Meta sends a webhook notification to the App that may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Any additional fields included in the lead form
  • Facebook Page and advertisement identifiers

How We Handle Data

The App temporarily receives lead data from Meta and immediately forwards it to the business associated with the Facebook or Instagram Page that created the advertisement.

The App does not store or retain personal data in any database or system after forwarding the lead information.

Purpose of Processing

The sole purpose of handling this data is to deliver lead information to the business that the user contacted through the advertisement so the business can respond to the user's inquiry.

Data Sharing

Lead information is forwarded only to the business that owns the Facebook or Instagram Page where the lead form was submitted.

We do not sell, rent, or share lead data with unrelated third parties.

Data Storage and Retention

The App does not store personal data. Lead information is transmitted through the webhook and immediately forwarded to the receiving business.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to ensure webhook communications are transmitted securely.

Your Rights

Since the App does not store personal data, we do not maintain user records within the application. However, users may:

  • Remove app permissions through Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Contact the business that received the lead to request deletion of their information
  • Contact Meta directly regarding data stored on their platform

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to individuals under the age of 18 and we do not knowingly process personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
