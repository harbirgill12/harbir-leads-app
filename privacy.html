Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how our application ("the App") handles data in connection with Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads. The App is operated by Harbir Gill ("we," "us," or "our").

What the App Does

The App is a webhook integration that receives lead notifications from Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads via the Meta platform. It acts solely as a pass-through — forwarding lead data to the associated business for follow-up. The App does not have any user-facing interface or user accounts.

Data We Receive

When a user submits a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta sends a webhook notification to our App containing:

  • Lead form submission details (name, email, phone number, and any other fields included in the lead form)
  • Facebook Page and Ad identifiers

How We Handle Your Data

The App does not store, retain, or process any personal data. It receives the webhook notification from Meta and immediately forwards the lead information to the associated business. No personal information is saved in our systems, databases, or logs.

Data Sharing

Lead data received via webhooks is forwarded to the business that owns the Facebook or Instagram page where the lead form was submitted. We do not sell, rent, or share data with any other third parties.

Data Storage & Retention

The App does not store any personal data. Since no data is retained, there is nothing to delete from our systems. The lead data exists only on Meta's platform and with the receiving business.

Your Rights

Since our App does not store any personal data, there is no user data within our application. However, you have the right to:

  • Remove our App's access via your Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites
  • Request deletion of data held by the business that received your lead — visit our Data Deletion page for instructions
  • Contact Meta directly regarding data on their platform

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect or receive personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
