Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

Introduction

Harbir Apps ("we", "us", or "our") operates this application integration (the "App") solely to facilitate the real-time delivery of lead information submitted through Meta Lead Ads on Facebook or Instagram associated with a business Page.

Scope and Applicable Law

This policy explains how information is handled in connection with the App and is designed to comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) as well as Meta Platform requirements.

PIPEDA governs the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information during commercial activities involving identifiable individuals.

Information We Handle

When a user submits a lead form on a Meta business Page, Meta sends a webhook notification to the App containing the information submitted in the form. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Custom form fields provided by the user

The App receives this information temporarily and forwards it to the business associated with the Facebook or Instagram Page that created the lead advertisement.

How Information Is Handled

The App acts only as a technical integration used to deliver lead information from Meta to the appropriate business.

The App does not:

  • Collect personal information directly from individuals
  • Store or retain personal information in databases, files, or long-term systems
  • Use the information for analytics, marketing, or profiling
  • Sell or share information with unrelated third parties

Information received through webhook notifications is transmitted to the business associated with the advertisement and is not retained after transmission.

Purpose of Processing

The sole purpose of handling this information is to enable prompt delivery of lead inquiries to the business associated with the advertisement so the business can respond to the request submitted through the lead form.

Data Sharing

Lead information is forwarded only to the business associated with the Facebook or Instagram Page where the lead form was submitted.

Transmission may occur through secure communication channels such as email services or CRM integrations used solely for delivery of the lead information.

Security

Although the App does not store personal data, reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect information while it is transmitted through webhook communications.

Openness and Transparency

This policy is made publicly available to explain how the App handles information in connection with Meta Lead Ads.

Individual Rights

Because the App does not retain personal information, it does not maintain records of lead submissions.

If you wish to access, correct, or delete information submitted through a lead form, you should contact the business associated with the advertisement or Meta directly.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
