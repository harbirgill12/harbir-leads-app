Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

Introduction

This page explains how individuals can request deletion of personal information submitted through Facebook or Instagram lead forms associated with Harbir Apps.

The App is used solely to facilitate the real-time delivery of lead information from Meta Lead Ads to the business associated with the advertisement.

Data Handling

When a user submits a lead form through Facebook or Instagram, Meta sends a webhook notification containing the submitted information to the App.

The App temporarily receives this information and forwards it to the business that created the advertisement.

The App does not store or retain personal information after it has been transmitted to the receiving business.

Requesting Data Deletion

Because the App does not retain personal data, it does not maintain records that can be deleted from its systems.

If you wish to request deletion of your information, you should contact the business associated with the advertisement where the lead form was submitted. That business is responsible for managing and deleting the information it receives.

You may also manage permissions or review connected apps through your Facebook or Instagram account settings.
