About Our App

Our Meta-integrated application is used solely to receive lead webhook notifications from Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads and forward them to the associated businesses. The app acts as a pass-through and does not store, retain, or process any personal data. Your lead information is handled directly by Meta and the business you submitted your inquiry to.

Your Right to Data Deletion

Since our app does not store any personal data, there is no user data within our application to delete. However, you can manage your data through the following options:

Option 1: Remove App Access via Facebook

You can revoke our app's access and remove any associated data through your Facebook settings:

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites
  2. Find and select our app from the list
  3. Click "Remove" to revoke access and request data deletion

Option 2: Contact the Business Directly

If you submitted a lead form and want the receiving business to delete your information, please contact them directly or reach out to us and we will connect you with the appropriate business.

Contact Us

Option 3: Email Us

For any data-related concerns, send an email to info@harbirgill.ca with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." Include your full name and the email or phone number you used so we can assist you.

What Happens After Your Request

  1. We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days
  2. Since our app does not store data, we will confirm that no data is held on our end
  3. If your data was forwarded to a business, we will coordinate with them to ensure deletion within 30 days
  4. You will receive a confirmation once the process is complete

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion or our privacy practices, please contact us at info@harbirgill.ca or visit our Privacy Policy page.
